# SSMLink Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 21, 2026*

SSMLink is an iPhone, iPad and Android app that reads diagnostic data from 1990s Subaru engine control
units through a Bluetooth adapter. This policy explains what the app does with your information.
The short version: **SSMLink doesn't collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.**

## What stays on your device

- **Engine readings, trouble codes and recordings.** Data read from your car's ECU is shown on
  your device. Recordings you choose to save are stored as CSV files in the app's own folder on
  your device (visible in the Files app). They never leave your device unless you share them
  yourself, for example with the Share button.
- **Settings.** Your selected parameters, address map and the last ROM ID read are stored on your
  device only.

## Bluetooth

SSMLink uses Bluetooth only to connect to the SSMLink adapter plugged into your car's
diagnostic connector. It doesn't use Bluetooth to locate you or to find other devices for any
other purpose.

On Android 8 to 11, the system requires the location permission before an app may scan for
Bluetooth Low Energy devices. SSMLink asks for it only for that reason: it never reads,
stores or sends your location. On Android 12 and later it uses the Nearby devices
permission, marked as never used for location.

## What we don't do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- No location data.
- No data is sent to us or to anyone else over the internet.

## Email

If you email us (for example from the About screen), we receive only what you choose to send,
and use it only to reply and fix problems. We don't share it.

## Children

SSMLink isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
